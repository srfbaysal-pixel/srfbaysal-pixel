# Şeref Baysal

I build AI-enabled products, automation systems, and software for real operational problems.

My background is in education and business management rather than traditional software engineering. I started building software because I repeatedly encountered operational problems that existing tools did not solve well enough. That gradually grew into designing and shipping full products, AI-assisted workflows, and automation systems.

Today, my work is focused on the intersection of **AI systems, product development, and business operations**.

## What I'm working on

I am currently developing systems around:

- AI-assisted decision making and workflow automation
- agentic workflows with controlled tool execution
- human-in-the-loop AI systems
- multi-provider LLM integration
- operational software for education businesses
- evaluation, reliability, and governance for AI agents

I care less about adding AI to a product for its own sake and more about whether the system is reliable, measurable, useful, and safe enough to operate in a real workflow.

## How I work

My projects usually start with an operational problem rather than a technology choice.

I define the workflow, identify where automation or AI actually adds value, design the system boundaries and failure conditions, and then iterate toward something that can be used outside a demo environment.

An increasing part of my work is focused on a question I think matters for production AI:

> How do we know the system works, and what happens when it doesn't?

That is why my current work is moving deeper into evaluation, observability, permissions, human approval, auditability, and failure handling.
